The problem

Exeter’s big nights sell out in minutes.

Club nights and society socials go on sale on a ticket page, often at a time that is only mentioned somewhere in the event description, and they are gone soon after. When a sold-out night quietly reopens because someone’s reservation lapsed, the window is about ninety seconds.

Then the spares go round group chats, where there is no way to know who you are paying or whether the ticket is real.

ExeTix fixes both halves. It tells you the second tickets drop, and when they have gone, it sells you a spare from a verified Exeter student.

What it does

Three things, done properly.

Drop alerts

Follow the clubs and societies you actually go to. Get pinged when a night is announced, ten minutes before a known release, the second it drops, and when a sold-out night restocks.

How the alerts work

Student resale

Every seller is verified with an @exeter.ac.uk email and lists under their real name. Sellers pay £0. Buyers pay a booking fee that only covers the card processing.

How resale works

Referrals that pay

A friend who uses your code gets £2 off booking fees. You get a free month, then £2 a month for every friend on a paid plan. Three friends cover your own.

Who it’s for

Exeter students, 18 and over.

Sellers verify an @exeter.ac.uk email and list under their real name, and the name on the ticket has to match the account. If a ticket fails at the door, a photo or video from the door gets the buyer a full refund.

ExeTix is independent. It is not affiliated with the University of Exeter, any venue, or any ticketing platform, and it never buys tickets on other platforms for you.
