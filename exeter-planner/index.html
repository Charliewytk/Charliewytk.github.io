Exeter Student Planner

An unofficial companion app for University of Exeter students. Your deadlines, modules, timetable and weekly to-dos in one calm place, built from the University’s learning site (ELE).

What it does

A to-do list that fills itself. Real deadlines from every module, plus each week’s slides, recordings, reading and tutorial prep, with reminders before each deadline.
Your modules, readable. Weeks, slides and recordings, with files you can save to read offline.
Private by design. You sign in on the University’s own Microsoft page; the app never sees your password. Your ELE data stays on your phone. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Help

I need an invite code. The app is invite-only. Ask a friend who has it for their code; each code works once. You get your own code to share after you unlock.

Just want to look around? On the “Connect to ELE” step, tap “Try with sample data (no login)”.
