Privacy policy

Exeter Student Planner · last updated 8 November 2026

Exeter Student Planner (“the app”) is an unofficial app made by an independent student. It is not affiliated with the University of Exeter.

What stays on your phone

Your ELE data (modules, deadlines, files, grades you enter), your to-dos, plans, gym log, settings and your ELE sign-in session (stored in the iOS Keychain). Signing out removes ELE data and sessions from the phone. We do not run a server that receives this data.

Sign-in

You sign in on the University’s Microsoft/ELE page inside the app. The app never sees or stores your password. The app talks directly from your phone to ELE (ele.exeter.ac.uk) and, if you choose, Talis reading lists.

Calendar

If you allow it, the app reads one calendar you pick to show your next lecture, and adds events you ask for. This stays on your phone.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone.

Invite codes

The app creates a random identifier on first launch (stored in the iOS Keychain on that phone only) and sends it to our invite service (Supabase) together with the app name. The service returns your 6-character invite code and records whether your code was used and whether you unlocked the app. When you enter a friend’s code, that code and your random identifier are sent to the service. No name, email, phone number or university details are sent. The service may log your IP address in standard server logs for security and rate limiting.

On-device features

Summaries and email triage use Apple’s on-device models; text is not sent to us.

What we don’t do

We do not sell data, show ads, track you across apps or use analytics. To delete invite-service data, contact charlie@wuytack.net; uninstalling the app and clearing its Keychain item removes the identifier on your phone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: charlie@wuytack.net.
