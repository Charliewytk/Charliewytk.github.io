Track 06 · macOS utility

PowerLeash

A low power mode that leaves the app in front alone.

Apple's low power mode made my Mac feel sluggish: it was cutting CPU performance by about 40% across the board. And on a 45 W charger, heavy work drained the battery while plugged in. So I made my own.

TRACK 06
59.0 → 42.7 Wsystem load, before and after Match Charger
−15.6 → −3.5 Wbattery flow while plugged in: from draining hard to nearly break-even
60 → 30 Wone heavy background app demoted; the app in front untouched

Two pieces

Eco Mode moves background apps onto the efficiency cores and leaves the app in front untouched. Match Charger caps total draw at what the charger can actually supply.

Holding the cap

Match Charger against a sustained 8-thread burn, system load per control cycle:

Burn starts57.8 W
Holding at the cap42.2 W
Still holding43.1 W
Burn ends, releasing11.3 W

Charger cap 42.8 W. Battery savings are not quantified yet.

Where it's at

  • Eco Mode: background apps to efficiency cores
  • Match Charger: caps total draw at what the charger supplies
  • Live power readout at 1 s, per-process watts
  • Fixed the power model double-counting the battery while charging
  • Quantify the actual battery saving; the gauge only updates every ~16 s
  • GPU-bound workloads: there's no public API to lower GPU clocks
Download for Mac

Apple Silicon, unsigned. Unzip, drag PowerLeash.app to /Applications, then right-click and Open. Double-click is blocked by Gatekeeper; that is expected.
