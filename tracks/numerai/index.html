Numerai

The audit, and a deadline that moves.

Three tournaments submitting on schedule. The model Numerai runs is byte-identical to the one in my repo, and it reproduces a live round's ranks exactly. After that, the only question is whether it's any good.

1.47Sharpe of CORR60 over 93 held-out eras, positive in 95% of them
1.27Sharpe of the payout score out of sample, with no losing quarter
2live rounds resolved so far. Too few to stake on; the policy asks for ten

Challengers and the gate

Eleven challengers were tested against the champion. The best, a blend with Numerai's benchmark, added 12% to the payout score but had a Holm-adjusted p of 0.14. It failed the gate and runs as an unstaked live A/B test instead.

Survivorship, measured

On Signals, scoring only the names I could map inflated correlation by +0.0109 (t 3.5). The fix was a point-in-time ticker map built from SEC fails-to-deliver data, which recovers 88% of dead names against 31% for the usual mapping service.

The deadline that moves

Every Crypto submission was silently ineligible for months: my log recorded success while the platform recorded late. The fix fires eight times across summer and winter time, takes a lock, checks whether the round is already in and refuses stale data. Uploads now land at 12:21:50Z, 41 minutes inside a deadline that drifts by about three minutes. Round 1341 was the first on time.
