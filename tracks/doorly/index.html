Track 05 · web + iOS

Doorly

The cheapest flight isn't the cheapest trip.

You only fly to get from A to B, but flight search sites don't count the cost and time of getting to and from the airport at either end. Doorly ranks the whole trip.

Door to door

A flight from Heathrow might cost £120 and one from City £130. A normal tracker recommends Heathrow because it's cheaper. From where I live, City works out cheaper door to door, once the trip to the airport is priced in.

Where it's at

  • Live provider data, door-to-door ranking, iOS build via Capacitor
  • Connecting flights: it only searches direct for now
  • Host it publicly rather than running it locally

The demo uses demo fares, labelled as such. The real thing pulls live prices.
