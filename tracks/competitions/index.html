WorldQuant BRAIN

Three days stuck at zero accepted submissions. It broke when I switched from single expressions to two-term combinations: correlation fell from 0.79 to 0.51 and fitness rose from 1.04 to 1.68. Gold followed, from a 416-simulation sweep. The components that made it work had each failed on their own.

Structural Break

Over three shipped models my holdout climbed from 57.31 to 58.75 while the live score went to 57.43. 263rd of 1,332. Levels agreeing says nothing about whether improvements transfer.
