The problem

Big nights in Exeter sell out in minutes, and the spares go round group chats with no way to know who you are paying.

What it does

ExeTix watches the clubs' and societies' ticket pages and pings you the second tickets go live. When they are gone, it sells you a spare from a verified Exeter student, priced at cost.

How it's built

A native SwiftUI app with a widget, Live Activities and rich pushes that carry the night's flyer. A Next.js marketplace on Stripe Connect. A two-machine Python watcher with a one-second fast lane around known release times, merged into one alert per drop.
