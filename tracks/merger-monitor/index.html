Annualising flatters everything

A 3% merger spread held to the median 49-day close annualises to 24.6% a year. Priced properly, it is +1.38% per deal: the same trade after a 2.4% break rate, a 27% median loss when one breaks, 0.40% round-trip cost and 0.49% of forgone T-bill carry.

Price a 1% spread the same way and it is −0.57% per deal. Base rate: 39 breaks in 1,606 settled outcomes, a floor rather than a ceiling.
