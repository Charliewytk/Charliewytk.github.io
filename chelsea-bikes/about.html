About Chelsea Bikes

Our story

Keeping Chelsea rolling since 2011

Chelsea Bikes is a trusted name in bicycle sales, repair and service. From our shop at 427 Kings Road we stock quality new bikes from brands like Raleigh, Ridgeback, Dawes, Pashley, Tern and more — alongside carefully refurbished used bikes at prices that make cycling accessible to everyone.

Sustainability is at the heart of what we do. Every reconditioned bike we sell is one saved from landfill; our workshop keeps hundreds of bikes on the road each year that might otherwise be scrapped. And through our close work with the police and the Police National Computer checks on every used bike we buy, we're proud to stand against bike theft in London.

Open seven days a week, with a committed, professionally trained workshop team — walk in any time.
