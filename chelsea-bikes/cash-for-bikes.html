Cash for bikes

Instant cash for your bicycle — a service most London bike shops don't offer. Free expert valuation, every purchase police-database checked.

How it works

Sold in four steps

Get an estimate

Send photos of your bike via the form below or WhatsApp for a quick estimated value, or just bring it straight in.

Free in-store valuation

Our expert valuer assesses age, condition, make and model and makes you our best offer on the spot.

Quick ID & security check

You must be 18+, fill in a short Owner's Declaration and show two forms of ID (one photo, one address). The bike is checked against the Police National Computer / CheckMEND.

Walk out with cash

Once cleared, you're paid the agreed value immediately — or trade the value for parts, accessories or servicing instead.

Anti-theft pledge

We stand against bike theft

We work closely with the police. Every bike we buy is run through the Police National Computer or CheckMEND before any payment is made — no exceptions.

Donations welcome

Unloved bike? Donate it

If your bike isn't worth selling, donate it — we'll find it a new home or reuse every good part. Greener than the skip.

Trade in

Swap value for anything in store

Use your bike's value against parts, accessories, servicing or another bike — often worth more than the cash.
