Cycle to Work scheme

Get a new bike and accessories through your employer and spread the cost from your salary before tax — typically saving 25–42%.

How it works

Tax-free cycling in four steps

Check with your employer

Ask HR if they're signed up to a Cycle to Work provider (Cyclescheme, Cycle2Work and others). Most large employers are.

Pick your bike with us

Come in and choose any bike and accessories — we'll size you up properly and put a quote together.

Get your voucher

Apply through your employer's scheme; they issue a voucher or certificate which you redeem with us.

Ride & save

Payments come out of your gross salary — you save the tax and National Insurance, usually 25–42% off.

Good to know

Frequently asked

What can I get?

Any bike in the shop — including e-bikes — plus safety accessories like helmets, locks and lights, up to your employer's scheme limit.

How much do I save?

Typically 25–33% for basic-rate taxpayers and up to 42% for higher-rate, spread over 12–18 monthly salary deductions.

Which schemes do you accept?

We work with the major Cycle to Work providers — call us on 020 7376 3700 to confirm yours before you apply.
