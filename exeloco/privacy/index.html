ExeLoco Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

ExeLoco is an invite-only app for University of Exeter students, run by Charles Wuytack ("we", "us"), who is the data controller under UK GDPR. Contact: charlie@wuytack.net. ExeLoco is not run by, or affiliated with, the University of Exeter.

What we collect

  • Account: your @exeter.ac.uk email address (used to send your login code) and an invite code you redeem or give out.
  • Profile: display name, year of study, course, home area, optional exact street (shown only to you and your friends), colour choice, and up to six profile photos.
  • Check-ins: the live camera photos (back and optional selfie) you post, which venue you are at, whether it is a pres or club check-in, and who can see it (friends, close friends or public).
  • Location, as a venue only: with your permission, your phone works out on the device which club or bar you are in and sends us only the venue. We never store coordinates, and friends never see your exact spot. Location can be used in the background to notice when you arrive at or leave a venue. Ghost mode stops sharing completely. We also record the nights you stayed at a club at least 30 minutes (night, venue and minutes) for streaks.
  • Social data: who you added or are friends with, close friends, blocks, message requests, squads you belong to, your "Out tonight?" answer and tonight's plans.
  • Messages: direct messages, squad chat messages and check-ins you share in them.
  • Safety reports: reports you send about a person, post or message, and your reason.
  • Settings: home-safe nudge settings, public leaderboard opt-in, and the time you last marked yourself home.
  • On your phone only: a cache of photos, and local notification choices. Reminders (such as the 7pm "Out tonight?" prompt) are scheduled on the phone and are not sent from our servers.

We do not collect your contacts, advertising identifier, browsing history or analytics, and we use no advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

To run the app: sign you in, show your profile and check-ins to the people you chose, deliver messages, work out streaks and keep people safe. Our basis is performing our contract with you (the Terms), plus consent for location and camera (you can withdraw it any time in iOS Settings or with Ghost mode), and our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe (reports, blocks and abuse prevention).

Who can see what

  • Friends are people who added each other. Only friends see your venue while you are out, and only you and friends see your exact street.
  • Check-ins are visible only to the audience you chose, and only until 06:00 (Exeter time) the same night, unless you save one to your profile. Public posts show the venue to strangers only after the night ends.
  • People you blocked and people who blocked you cannot see you or message you.
  • Access rules are enforced on our database itself, not just in the app.

Who we share data with

We do not sell data. We use one processor, Supabase, which hosts our database, file storage and login emails. Our project runs in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany, region eu-central-1). Supabase and its sub-processors may handle data outside the UK/EU under standard contractual clauses. Venue names and outlines come from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Check-ins you did not save disappear from the app at 06:00 (Exeter time) the same night, and their photos are deleted from our storage periodically after that. Presence (your live venue) is only used for a few hours and is cleared when you sign out or turn on Ghost mode. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, check-ins, messages, friend links and invite data are deleted straight away and cannot be recovered. Reports about others may be kept for a limited period to protect the community.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, and object to processing. Most of this is in the app: edit your profile, Ghost mode, Blocked users, and Settings > Delete account. For anything else, email charlie@wuytack.net. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Age

ExeLoco is for adults (18+) who are students at the University of Exeter. We do not knowingly collect data from under-18s; tell us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored in access-controlled private storage. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you think your account is at risk.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
