ExeLoco

ExeLoco is an invite-only app for University of Exeter students. See which friends are out tonight, post live check-ins that disappear at 6am, plan the night with your squad and keep your night-out streak going.

How it works

Exeter students, 18 and over. Sign up with your @exeter.ac.uk email and a login code we email you. You need an invite code from a friend who already has the app.
Venue, never your exact spot. Your phone turns your location into a venue name. Friends see the venue, never coordinates. Ghost mode hides you completely.
Safety. Report or block anyone from the “…” menu on any profile, post or message. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Help

I didn’t get my login code. Check your university email’s junk folder, then try again after a minute.

I need an invite code. Ask a friend who already has ExeLoco. Each code unlocks 3 friends.

Delete my account. Me > gear icon (Settings) > Delete account. This removes your profile, photos and messages.
