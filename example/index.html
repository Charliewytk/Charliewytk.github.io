Worked example · not advice

A 3% spread annualises to 24.6%. After costs it is +1.38%.

On the 19 August 2026 week-late table, ACA (Arcosa, Inc.) has a contractual cash price of $150.00 and a market price of $145.01. That is a filing and a price, not a return and not a recommendation.

The method, in order

  1. Extract. Read the cash figure from the definitive proxy. The sentence is below. If the filing does not state one cash number, the row says so and stops.
  2. Price. Offer over market, minus one: $150.00 / $145.01 − 1 = +3.44%.
  3. Annualise. ACA is close to the site’s 3% case, so the published 3% figures are used. Held to the median 49-day close, 3% looks like 24.6% a year.
  4. Charge the stated break rate and costs. A 2.4% break rate, a 27% median loss when one breaks, 0.40% round-trip cost, and 0.49% of forgone T-bill carry.
  5. Net. After that pass the 3% spread is +1.38% a deal. A 1% spread is −0.57%. Break-even is 1.59%.
