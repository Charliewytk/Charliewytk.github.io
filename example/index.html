The method, in order

  1. Extract. Read the cash figure from the definitive proxy. The sentence is below. If the filing does not state one cash number, the row says so and stops.
  2. Price. Divide offer by the market at build time. ACA is +3.44% on that dated table.
  3. Annualise a 3% spread. Held to the median 49-day close, 3% looks like 24.6% a year. That is the number this is usually sold on. ACA is that 3% case in the wild; the published arithmetic stays the 3% case already used on /subscribe/ and the homepage. A new expected value is not invented for +3.44%.
  4. Charge the stated break rate and costs. A 2.4% break rate, a 27% median loss when one breaks, 0.40% round-trip cost, and 0.49% of forgone T-bill carry.
  5. The net already on this site. After that pass the same 3% spread is +1.38% a deal. A 1% spread is −0.57%. Break-even is 1.59%.
