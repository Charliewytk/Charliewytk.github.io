Why a 1% spread dies

The break rate is 2.4% after a definitive proxy — 39 of 1,606 settled outcomes. It is a floor, not a ceiling: deals that die before that stage never enter the sample. Carry is the 13-week bill, fetched rather than assumed. US/GBP round-trip FX and spread are charged in the same pass.

That is the whole method: the annualised number is marketing. The expected value is the trade. Nothing here is investment advice. No position is held in anything listed.
