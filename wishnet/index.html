Never wait for a washer again

WishNet works with Washnet laundries in student halls. Press one button and it reserves the next washer the moment it frees up, counts down your wash on your Lock Screen, and grabs a dryer 5 minutes before your wash ends.

How it works

Your own Washnet account. You log in to Washnet inside the app. WishNet reserves machines exactly as if you tapped “Make reservation” yourself. You still pay at the machine as normal.
Keep it open while it hunts. iPhone pauses apps in the background, so leave WishNet on screen while it’s waiting for a machine. Countdowns and reminders keep working with the app closed.
One machine at a time. WishNet never reserves a second washer or dryer while you already hold one.

Help

It says I’m not logged in. Open the ⋯ menu, choose Log in to Washnet, and log in with Google, Apple or your Washnet email.

My hall isn’t listed. WishNet lists every site Washnet shows on its website. If yours is missing, email us.
