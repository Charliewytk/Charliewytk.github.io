Privacy policy

WishNet · last updated 7 November 2026

The short version

WishNet does not collect, store or share your personal data. There is no WishNet account, no WishNet server holding your details, no analytics and no advertising.

Your Washnet login

You log in on Washnet’s own login page, shown inside the app. Your password goes straight to Washnet; WishNet never sees or stores it. Washnet’s login cookie is kept on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage, so WishNet can check machines and make reservations for you. Deleting the app removes it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The laundry site you chose.
  • Timers for your own machines (wash, dryer and 10-minute reservation holds).
  • The initials Washnet shows on your reservations (for example “Reserved AB”), so WishNet can recognise which machine is yours.
  • A small diagnostic log, which never leaves your phone unless you send it to us yourself.

Who WishNet talks to

Only Washnet (washnet.co.uk), to read machine status and make reservations you asked for. Reminders and Lock Screen countdowns are created on your iPhone by Apple’s system; nothing is sent to us.

Children

WishNet is meant for students using Washnet laundries and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact
