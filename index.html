Chess Exchange

A play-money prediction market on real chess.com games. When titled players start a blitz or bullet game, a market opens with White/Draw/Black shares priced from an Elo and draw-rate prior; when the real game ends, the official result resolves it and winning shares pay 100¢. A logarithmic market scoring rule means there's always a price to trade against, six liquidity bots with different aggression and noise profiles trade on material and clock, and just-finished games are rebroadcast move-by-move at true clock speed with a live board — so prices swing on blunders like the real thing. No frameworks, no real money.

LMSR market maker · 6 bots · live titled-player games

Runs as a live server, so it can't be embedded in this static site — a hosted demo is coming.

Abyss Warden

An original deep-sea tower defence game: harpoon turrets, arc coils and drone bays against armoured isopods, cloaked stalkers and five trench-shaking bosses. Every sprite is drawn in canvas code and every sound is synthesised live with the Web Audio API — no engine, no dependencies, no borrowed assets. Doorly

The cheapest flight isn't always the cheapest trip. Doorly is a flight scanner that ranks journeys by true door-to-door cost: transport from your exact location to the airport, the fare, then transport to your exact accommodation. Every leg's mode — tube, train, express, bus, taxi, bike — is selectable with its own cost and time, and the ranking updates live. The full version runs on live provider data with an iOS build via Capacitor; the demo here runs on clearly-labelled demo fares.

PowerLeash

A selective battery saver for Apple Silicon Macs. It began as an attempt to stop Low Power Mode dropping the display to 60 Hz — then measurement showed it never does: the panel stays at 120 Hz while the CPU loses up to 40% of its speed, which is exactly why Low Power Mode feels sluggish. So instead of a blunt power cut, PowerLeash demotes background apps to the efficiency cores through the public Darwin scheduling API and leaves the app you're actually looking at at full speed. Verified on processes it didn't spawn, with a clean restore.

measured: 717 → 210 Mops/s on demote, 713 on restore

Research

Hypothesis Factory

Backtesting infrastructure with four enforced non-negotiables: pre-registration before data is touched, automatic false-discovery-rate correction, a sealed one-shot holdout, and no parameter sweeping — each enforced by code, not policy.

Verdicts filed

Strategy Validation

Five capacity-constrained retail trading strategies validated on free data with costs included and biases stamped. Final verdicts: one promising upper bound, one marginal-but-real, three dead — each with the specific number that killed it.
