Things I've made. Most of them work right here on this page.

Abyss Warden

Tower defence game set on the ocean floor. ~5,600 lines of vanilla JS, no engine, no assets: sprites are drawn in code, sounds synthesised in the browser. Five bosses.

Play it — free, no install, phones work.

Doorly

Flight search that prices the journey door to door, tube/train/taxi legs included, not just the fare. iOS build exists.

Try the demo (fake fares, labelled as such — the real thing pulls live prices)

Chelsea Bikes

Rebuilt chelseabikes.co.uk to pitch to the shop. Their site has the phone number stuck inside an image; mine has their real prices and works offline. Not pitched yet.

Before/after · full prototype

Chess Exchange

Prediction market on live chess.com games. When titled players start a blitz game a market opens on the result, an LMSR market maker sets prices, six bots trade off material and clock, and the real result settles it. Play money. Runs as a server, so no web demo yet — screenshots soon.

PowerLeash

Mac menu bar app. I measured Low Power Mode and found it throttles the CPU ~40% without touching the display refresh rate, so I built this instead: background apps get pushed onto the efficiency cores, the app in front stays fast.

background app: 717 → 210 Mops/s
frontmost app: untouched
restore: clean, 713 Mops/s

Download (Apple Silicon, unsigned — right click → Open)

Trading research

A backtesting rig with pre-registration, a one-shot holdout and FDR correction. Ran 90 ideas through it and all 90 failed, which I wrote up: 90 Hypotheses, Zero Edges (pdf). Also five small-account strategies taken to written verdicts (three dead), and live tournament models — the DataCrunch one is around 14th of 13,534.
