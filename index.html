Flight search · web and ios

Doorly

One only flies to get from point A to point B. But I found that websites such as flightscanner did not account for the prices and time taken to get to and from the airport on either side.

For example, a flight from Heathrow may cost £120 and from City £130. Of which normal flight trackers would recommend Heathrow as it is cheaper. However, taking into account where you live, at least for me, City would end up being cheaper.

Try the demo Demo fares, labelled as such — the real thing pulls live prices.

Where it's at

  • Live provider data, door-to-door ranking, iOS build via Capacitor
  • Connecting flights — right now it only searches direct
  • Host it publicly rather than running locally

Mac menu bar app · swift

PowerLeash

A custom low power mode. Apple's native low power mode made macOS feel sluggish: the cursor was slow and laggy and the movements felt off.

I also only have a charger of 45W on a Mac that can draw much more than that: I was losing battery working on heavy tasks while on charge.

I found out the reason LPM was sluggish was because it killed the CPU by about 40%.

eco mode · background app: 717 → 210 Mops/s
frontmost app: untouched

match charger on a 45W adapter:
system load: 59.0 → 42.7 W
battery flow: −15.6 → −3.5 W

Eco Mode is my own low power mode, moving all background apps to efficiency cores leaving the foreground untouched. Match Charger capped my total draw by what I could supply.

Download for Mac Apple Silicon, unsigned — right click → Open.

Where it's at

  • Eco Mode — background apps to efficiency cores
  • Match Charger — caps total draw at what the charger supplies
  • Live power readout at 1s, per-process watts, brightness break-even
  • Fixed the power model double-counting battery while charging
  • Quantify the actual battery saving — the gauge only updates every ~16s
  • GPU-bound workloads: no public API to lower GPU clocks
