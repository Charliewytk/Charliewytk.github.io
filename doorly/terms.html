Terms & disclaimer

Last updated 10 August 2026 · Doorly is in beta

Every number on Doorly is an estimate, not a quote. Nothing shown here is an offer to sell you a flight, and no price is guaranteed until an airline or travel agent confirms it at the point of booking.

What Doorly does

Doorly compares the whole journey — getting to the departure airport, the fare, and getting from the arrival airport to where you are staying — so that a cheap fare from a far-away airport does not look better than it is. It is a comparison tool. It does not sell flights, take payment, hold bookings, or act as a travel agent.

Beta

Doorly is in beta and provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. It may be wrong, incomplete, or unavailable without notice. Do not rely on it as the only basis for booking travel, and always confirm fares, times and baggage rules with the airline before you pay.

Direct flights only

Doorly searches direct flights only. Journeys with connections are not searched, so a route Doorly reports as having no flights may well be reachable with a change. It also searches a limited set of airports near each end, not every airport in the world.

How to read the prices

  • Fares come from flight data providers. Depending on the source they are either live availability or indicative prices recently found by other travellers — the pill above the results always says which. Indicative fares may already be gone. Either way, prices move constantly and the fare can change before you click through.
  • Some sources publish a departure time but no arrival time. Where that happens, the arrival and flight duration are estimated from the distance between the airports and marked with a "~". The departure time is always as published.
  • Ground transport costs and times are modelled estimates, not live prices. Public transport figures come from published fares and typical journey times.
  • Taxi and rideshare figures are rough estimates built from typical local rates. Real fares vary with traffic, time of day, surge pricing and route, and can differ substantially. They are labelled as estimates throughout the app.
  • Airport waiting time is a fixed allowance, not advice about how early you personally should arrive.
  • Prices are shown in GBP. Where a fare is sold in another currency the converted figure is indicative, and your card issuer's rate and fees will differ.
  • Baggage is shown only when a provider declares it. Where nothing is declared, Doorly says so rather than guessing — check the airline.
  • "Best value" ranks using a fixed notional value for your travel time. It is a convenience, not a financial judgement about what your time is worth.

Some booking links are affiliate links, and Doorly may earn a commission if you book through one. This never affects how results are ranked and never adds anything to the price you pay. Rankings are produced only by the cost and time figures described above.

Doorly gives no travel, visa, immigration, insurance, health or financial advice. Checking passports, visas, entry rules and travel insurance is yours to do.
